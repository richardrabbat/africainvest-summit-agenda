Good to know

Weather

Mediterranean climate: warm days of 27 to 35°C (81 to 95°F) and cooler evenings around 19 to 22°C (66 to 72°F).

Light clothing, plus a light jacket or blazer for air-conditioned rooms. Showers are possible, so a small umbrella is worth packing.

Time zone
Central European Time, GMT+1, no daylight saving.
Electricity
230 V, 50 Hz, plug types C and E (European two-pin round). Bring a travel adapter if your devices use a different plug.
Currency

Tunisian Dinar (TND), a closed currency that cannot be exchanged before arrival.

ATMs are widely available in Tunis and take international cards. Major credit cards work in hotels and larger establishments; carry some cash for smaller vendors and taxis.

Dress code
Casual attire.
